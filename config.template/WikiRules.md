## Structure

The wiki has these top-level folders, each with an `index.md`:

- `me/`: the author's own growth, feedback, goals and achievements.
- `people/`: anyone with a relationship to the author, one folder per person: `people/{first-name-last-name}/index.md`.
- `projects/`: initiatives with a defined scope and goal.
- `learning/`: knowledge gained or referenced.
- `plans/`: time-boxed intentions.
- `decisions/`: choices made or being weighed, with their rationale.
- `reflections/`: processing an experience or feeling.

File each piece of knowledge in the folder that matches its strongest relation to the author, and link to it from
the other places it is relevant.

## Pages

- Every folder has an `index.md` that summarises and links to its pages and sub-folders.
- Link related pages to each other with relative markdown links.
- Create a page for a subject once there is enough to say about it; until then, add the fact to the most relevant
  existing page.

## Naming

- File and folder names are kebab case, e.g. `jane-doe/` or `move-to-new-team.md`.
- Dates in names and content use ISO format: `yyyy-mm-dd`.
